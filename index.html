Gig Worker Cybersecurity Best Practices

Essential defense rules every delivery partner and driver must know.

✅ Gig Worker Security DOs
  • 1. Use In-App Support Only: Always resolve order cancellations and disputes via the official gig app help section.
  • 2. Delivery OTP after Handover: Only share the delivery confirmation code after the package is physically in customer hands.
  • 3. Enable App Screen Lock: Set a biometric fingerprint or PIN lock on your phone and payment apps.
  • 4. Report immediately: If you suspect fraud, dial 1930 (National Cyber Helpline) right away.
🛑 Gig Worker Security DONTs
  • 1. NEVER Scan QR to Receive Money: In India UPI, receiving money NEVER requires entering your PIN or scanning a code.
  • 2. NEVER Share Bank / Login OTP: Official representatives from Zomato, Swiggy, Uber, or banks will NEVER ask for your OTP.
  • 3. Avoid Auto-Accept APKs: Never download unofficial 'batch grabber' or auto-clicker APKs from Telegram groups.
  • 4. Never Use Public Wi-Fi for Banking: Unsecured roadside Wi-Fi can expose your banking passwords.
💡

The Golden Rule of UPI for Gig Workers

Remember this during customer delivery tips and refund requests:

When Receiving Money:

You only share your Phone Number / UPI ID. You NEVER scan any QR code and NEVER enter your UPI PIN.

When Sending Money:

You scan QR and enter UPI PIN. Entering your PIN ALWAYS deducts money from your account!
