Gig Worker Cybersecurity Best Practices

Essential rules every delivery partner, cab driver, and freelancer in India must know.

✅ Gig Worker Security DOs
  • 1. kse In-App Support Only: Always resolve order cancellations and address disputes via the official gig app help section.
  • 2. Delivery OTP after Handover: Only share the delivery confirmation code after the package is physically handed over to the customer.
  • 3. Enable App Screen Lock: Set a biometric fingerprint or PIN lock on your phone and payment apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm).
  • 4. Report immediately: If you suspect fraud, dial 1930 (National Cybercrime Helpline) right away.
❥ Gig Worker Security DONTs
  • 1. NEVER Scan QR to Receive Money: In India's UPI system, receiving money NEVER requires entering your UPI PIN or scanning a code.
  • 2. NEVER Share Bank / Login OTP: Official representatives from Zomato, Swiggy, Uber, or banks will NEVER ask for your OTP.
  • 3. Avoid Auto-Accept APKs: Never download unofficial 'batch grabber' or auto-clicker A>Ks from Telegram or WhatsApp groups.
  • 4. Never Use Public Wi-Fi for Payments: Unsecured restaurant or roadside Wi-Fi can expose your banking passwords.
